Running since 2006 with full licensing and stable rules: modest pay, dependable operation.

In this corner of the Chinese internet, yidiaowang has the longest history: online since January 2006 as "First Survey Network", renamed in 2019. Its operator, Shanghai Kurun, is a market research firm holding a foreign-related survey licence and a municipal safe-site rating. Credentials do not guarantee a good experience, but they do indicate a company that is not going to disappear overnight.

The product logic is the classic panel loop: register, fill in a detailed profile, wait for invitations, complete surveys, accumulate points, redeem. Inventory is consumer research across FMCG, appliances, cars, finance and media habits. Because the operator runs commissioned studies, some questionnaires come from real brand clients and are more substantive than pure aggregated traffic.

Earnings are unremarkable. Per-survey rewards run from a few yuan to a dozen, and a month of steady work usually produces 100 to 300 CNY. Its value is not the rate but the consistency: rules unchanged for over a decade and no large-scale payout failures, which for a low-effort side activity is itself worth something.

Two things to expect: the interface and flow are traditional, and registration asks for a lot of profile detail, which is also why matching is relatively precise. It also runs a member integrity system, so careless answering leads to downranking or removal.

Strengths

  • Operating since 2006 with full licensing and stability
  • The operator is a research firm, so some studies come from real brands
  • Rules have stayed stable rather than churning
  • Detailed profiles improve matching accuracy

Weaknesses

  • Dated interface with a weak mobile experience
  • Low unit price; a typical month is 100 to 300 CNY
  • Long registration questionnaire
  • Invitation frequency depends on quotas, not constant

Practical tips

  • Complete the profile properly; it drives matching
  • Answer at a natural pace, since the integrity system logs anomalies
  • Watch for invitation emails, as popular studies are first-come
  • Treat it as a long-term account rather than a quick win

Frequently asked

Is yidiaowang the same as First Survey Network?

Yes. It was renamed in 2019 and the operator, Shanghai Kurun, is unchanged.

How long do payouts take?

Redemptions follow the site's internal process, usually several business days. Check the account page for current tiers.

Why am I getting fewer surveys?

Usually quota saturation, reduced account activity, or a hit to your integrity score. Regular logins and careful answers help.
