A clear 1000-points-to-10-yuan rate, mixing surveys with cashback tasks.

shoujiang's virtue is bluntness: 1,000 points equals 10 yuan, no more, no less. There is no conversion puzzle and no tier system to decode. For anyone new to this category, being able to understand the rules at a glance matters more than a marginally better rate.

Tasks are mixed. Surveys are the main engine, supplemented by a daily check-in (typically 2 points), shopping cashback and product trials. Check-in points are trivial and will not fund anything, but the daily click keeps the account active, which indirectly affects survey allocation.

Pacing needs honesty. One documented test had a new user complete four surveys and bank 944 points on the first afternoon, which looks like a decent hourly rate; the next day brought a single invitation, which was then screened out. That first-day-then-drought pattern is common to aggregated panels and not specific to this site.

The right usage is therefore a fixed ten-to-twenty minute check rather than a daily grind: take what matches, close the tab otherwise. A month typically lands between 50 and 150 CNY, and expecting it to cover living costs will disappoint.

Strengths

  • Transparent conversion at 1,000 points to 10 yuan
  • A 10-yuan floor, which is moderately low
  • Varied tasks beyond surveys, including cashback
  • Daily check-in keeps the account active

Weaknesses

  • Highly variable inventory, with dry days after a good first day
  • Effective rate of only 5 to 10 CNY per hour
  • Check-in tasks are worth almost nothing
  • Mixing cashback with surveys invites misallocated time

Practical tips

  • Do surveys; judge cashback tasks separately on actual value
  • Check once a day rather than refreshing repeatedly
  • Prioritise tasks whose points clearly exceed the time ratio
  • Redeem at 10 yuan instead of accumulating

Frequently asked

Is 1,000 points really 10 yuan?

That is the published rate; actual redemption tiers are shown in your account, and some campaigns add conditions.

Why did surveys dry up the next day?

Quotas, profile fit and advertiser pacing all matter. Supply volatility is normal on aggregated panels and does not mean your account was restricted.

How long do withdrawals take?

Usually a few business days per the site's own terms, and the first one may require extra real-name details.
