Chinese survey, research and crowdsourcing platforms

This group settles in CNY to WeChat Pay or Alipay, often with thresholds as low as 1 yuan. Unit prices are small but payout friction is minimal, which makes them pocket-money tools. Note the split: survey panels where you answer questions, and crowdsourcing platforms where you label, transcribe or moderate, the latter paying more but requiring you to learn the specs.

Verified：November 2026
Caution: the market is full of "reward task" apps that mix surveys with app-install, referral and voting tasks, which carry materially higher risk through account authorisation and identity exposure. This page lists only platforms with an identifiable operator and published rules.
