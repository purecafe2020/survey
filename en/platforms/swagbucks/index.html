Swagbucks

An SB points ecosystem where surveys are just one track, alongside cashback and small tasks.

International Overall score：4.0 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Swagbucks is the broadest platform in this category: surveys, shopping cashback, videos, search and trial sign-ups all convert into SB points, where 1 SB is about one cent. The upside is optionality, since you can switch tracks when surveys dry up. The downside is that it quietly encourages low-value activity, and an honest hourly calculation at the end of the week can be ugly.

If your goal is to convert spending you already do into points, Swagbucks beats most pure survey panels: buying through its links earns faster than an hour of questionnaires. If your goal is straight cash, stick to surveys and a handful of high-value tasks and ignore the video treadmill.

On payouts, gift cards start at roughly $3 while PayPal thresholds are higher and vary by country, so check the figure shown inside your account rather than the marketing page. Redemptions usually land within a few business days to two weeks, with gift cards generally faster.

The fit is specific: people who already shop online and are happy to collect cashback along the way. If you never shop online and just want a clean pay-per-survey experience, Survey Junkie or Prime Opinion are a better match.

Strengths

  • Widest range of earning tracks in one account
  • Cashback stacking can be worth more than surveys alone
  • Low gift-card threshold, from about $3
  • Operating for over a decade with a traceable payment record

Weaknesses

  • Lots of low-value tasks that quietly destroy your hourly rate
  • PayPal thresholds are higher than gift cards and vary by country
  • Survey screen-out rate is on the high side
  • The points system makes it easy to misjudge your real hourly pay

Practical tips

  • Stick to surveys and clearly priced tasks; skip the video grind
  • Route online shopping through its cashback link, which beats survey pay
  • Convert SB to dollars before deciding whether a task is worth it
  • Pick gift cards you will genuinely use rather than reselling them

Frequently asked

Is Swagbucks available in China?

No. Its supported markets are mainly North America, Europe and Australia, and accounts from mainland China generally see no survey inventory.

How much is 1 SB worth?

About one cent, so 100 SB is roughly $1. Gift card denominations can shift the effective rate slightly.

Why is my cash-out threshold different from what I read?

PayPal thresholds vary by country and account status, while marketing pages quote the lowest gift-card tier. Trust the redemption page inside your own account.
