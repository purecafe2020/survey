International survey and research platforms

What these share: they require genuine residence, mostly support specific countries, and pay through PayPal or bank transfer. Rates are generally higher than Chinese panels, but they are largely unavailable to users in mainland China, and we will not be vague about that.

Verified：November 2026
Compliance note: using proxies to fake a region or buying an overseas identity breaches platform terms. Bans forfeit balances and expose personal data to resale. The information below documents how the market works.
