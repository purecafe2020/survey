阿里众包

Crowdsourcing inside the Alibaba ecosystem, with fast payouts and standardised tasks.

Alibaba Zhongbao sources its work from Alibaba's own operations: Taobao catalogue checks, image annotation, voice evaluation and merchant credential verification. These tasks are standardised. Every item has a defined right answer, so you apply rules rather than exercise judgement or talk to clients.

Settlement is the strong point. Rewards go to Alipay, usually instantly or same day, with no review cycle. For people who need money to land today, that practical immediacy beats a higher headline rate paid monthly.

The weak points are unit price and repetition. Catalogue checks often pay a few mao per item, so the hourly rate is built from volume. Voice evaluation pays more, five to ten yuan per item, but demands stricter equipment and environment. With practice expect 10 to 20 CNY an hour.

One thing to respect: accuracy requirements are hard. Too many errors and your access is throttled or revoked. Do the first few dozen items carefully to build an accuracy record, because that record determines how much work you see later.

Strengths

  • Fast Alipay payouts, often instant or same day
  • Standardised tasks with clear rules
  • Steady supply from Alibaba's own operations
  • No specialist skills needed, just care

Weaknesses

  • Low unit price, so income comes from volume
  • Repetitive and fatiguing
  • Strict accuracy scoring that can throttle access
  • Some task types require a desktop rather than a phone

Practical tips

  • Read the task examples and understand the criteria before batching
  • Slow down for the first few dozen items to build accuracy
  • Prefer categories you already know, which speeds judgement
  • Cash out on a fixed rhythm instead of accumulating

Frequently asked

How do I access Alibaba Zhongbao?

Through the Taobao or Alipay app search entries, or the official site. Some task types require real-name verification and training.

Are payouts instant?

Most rewards land in Alipay instantly or same day, per the platform's settlement notes.

Why can't I get tasks?

Likely accuracy below threshold, no inventory in your region, or missing training for that task family.
