Branded Surveys

A long-running North American panel where your tier changes payout speed and rates.

International Overall score：3.8 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Branded Surveys is an old-school panel with a conventional loop: sign up, complete your profile, take surveys, collect points, cash out. What makes it slightly different is the tier system, where doing more surveys with a good pass rate moves you from Bronze to Silver to Gold, and the tier changes both payout speed and some survey rates.

That design rewards consistent, honest users. Rushing through surveys and getting flagged as invalid lowers your tier and your rates. If you intend to do this seriously over months, the tiering works in your favour compared with one-off accounts.

Set expectations on the hourly rate. Two independent tests in 2026 calculated $1.47 and $2.20 per hour, which is on the low side. Surveys run five to twenty minutes, so the per-task reward is small though volume is reasonable. If you are in the US, Canada or the UK and you are not chasing the highest possible rate, it earns a place as a filler in your rotation.

One thing people miss: the site mixes in sweepstake-style surveys where completion enters you into a monthly draw. Do not count those as income; they are entertainment. Standard surveys are what actually pay.

Strengths

  • $5 cash-out floor
  • Tier system rewards long-term consistent users
  • Long track record of paying
  • PayPal, bank transfer and gift cards all supported

Weaknesses

  • Low hourly rate, tested at $1.50 to $2.20
  • Mainly North America and the UK
  • Sweepstake surveys blur the picture of real earnings
  • Losing a tier degrades both rates and payout speed

Practical tips

  • Answer carefully to protect your tier; speed is not worth a downgrade
  • Track sweepstake entries separately from real earnings
  • Cash out at $5 rather than holding a balance
  • A complete profile meaningfully increases survey volume

Frequently asked

Is Branded Surveys available outside North America?

Not in practice. Inventory concentrates in North America and the UK, so accounts elsewhere see almost nothing.

What happens if my tier drops?

Instant payouts revert to 1-2 business days of review and some survey rates decrease. You recover the tier by consistently completing surveys.

Why was my survey closed mid-way?

Either you did not match the target demographic or the system flagged an unusual answering pattern. Such closures are usually unpaid.
