YouGov

Genuine polling work rather than product questionnaires: modest pay, high survey quality.

International Overall score：3.7 / 5 Verified：November 2026

What separates YouGov is its identity. It is a London-listed research company that has spent decades running opinion and consumer polls for media, brands and academics, and much of the "X% of people think..." data quoted in news comes from panels like this. Your answers actually enter a statistical sample rather than an aggregated traffic pool.

The trade-off is low pay. Per-survey rewards are modest and invitations are sparse; some weeks bring one or two. Third-party estimates put the effective rate at roughly $2 to $3 per hour. If you are genuinely interested in taking part in real polling, though, the question quality is clearly above the average product survey: you get asked about policy, media trust and brand perception, which is harder to get bored by.

Cash-out is the weak spot. Thresholds differ sharply by country, with some markets requiring the equivalent of $15 to $50, which can take months. The realistic framing is a long-term account you top up occasionally, not a short-term earning tool.

One underrated benefit is longitudinal consistency: the same questions recur over time, which reveals how opinion shifts. For anyone writing analysis or content, that first-hand sample has value beyond the cash.

Strengths

  • Run by a listed research company; answers enter real samples
  • High-quality questions on social issues and brands
  • Wide country coverage including parts of Asia-Pacific
  • Comparable longitudinal data, useful for research and writing

Weaknesses

  • High thresholds in several countries, sometimes months of accumulation
  • Sparse invitations make earnings irregular
  • Effective rate of about $2 to $3, well below Prolific
  • Unsuitable if you want cash quickly

Practical tips

  • Complete your profile fully, especially region and media habits, which drive invitations
  • Treat it as a long-term account rather than a quick cash-out
  • Check your country's specific threshold before setting expectations
  • The polling data itself is often worth more than the payout

Frequently asked

Is YouGov available in mainland China?

YouGov runs dozens of country sites independently, and mainland China is not a standard market. Check whether your country's site offers registration rather than faking a region.

Why does cashing out take so long?

Thresholds are set per country and invitations are infrequent, so many users need months to reach the minimum.

Is my data published?

Results are released as aggregate statistics, not individual responses. See the official privacy policy for the binding detail.
