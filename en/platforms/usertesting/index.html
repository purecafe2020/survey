UserTesting

Unmoderated usability tests: think out loud while clicking, about 20 minutes each.

Deep research Overall score：4.0 / 5 Verified：November 2026

The work is concrete: a client hands you a website or app, asks you to complete a few tasks, and wants your thoughts out loud while you do it. You record screen and voice for about twenty minutes and hand back a user's-eye critique. No technical skill required, only the willingness to narrate as you click.

At $10-60 a test it pays less than Respondent, but the advantage is frequency and friction: no interview to schedule, no appointment, do it whenever. For someone new to paid research, it is the easiest loop to complete end to end.

Hardware is a real gate. The platform asks for at least 8GB of RAM, a 1920×1080 screen and 5Mbps upload on desktop, and 20% battery plus the app on mobile. Fall short and the recording fails mid-way, which means no payment.

The most common mistake is being too smooth. Clients want genuine friction: the button you could not find, the label you misread, the moment you hesitated. Clicking through while saying everything is fine gets flagged as low quality. Slow down and narrate where you got stuck.

Strengths

  • No interview, start any time, the easiest entry point
  • A clear twenty-minute time cost per test
  • No specialist background needed, just a real user's view
  • Transparent PayPal settlement

Weaknesses

  • Lower per-session pay than interview platforms
  • Hardware shortfalls cause mid-test failure and no pay
  • Fourteen-day settlement is slow
  • Requires recorded screen and voice, which rules out the privacy-averse

Practical tips

  • Run the equipment check first to confirm mic and resolution
  • Narrate continuously; silent clicking gets rejected
  • Pause and talk through confusion, which is exactly what clients want
  • Do one task at a time rather than rushing ahead

Frequently asked

What hardware do I need?

On desktop, 8GB RAM, a 1920×1080 screen and at least 5Mbps upload; on mobile, the official app and adequate battery. Shortfalls cause failed recordings.

Why was my test rejected?

Usually silent audio, abandoning tasks, overly brief narration, or no genuine friction expressed. Low quality scores reduce future invitations.

Is it usable from mainland China?

You can register, but inventory is mostly English-language markets and requires stable access to overseas sites and PayPal.
