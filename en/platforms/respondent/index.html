Respondent

A per-session research marketplace where the screener is unpaid and the session pays real money.

Respondent is not a survey site; it is a research recruitment marketplace. Researchers, mostly SaaS companies, product teams at large firms and consultancies, post studies with the format, length and incentive stated. You apply, answer a screener, and if selected you get booked into the session. The economics are therefore different: income comes from how often you are selected multiplied by the session fee, not from volume.

Payouts are the highest in this category. Its help centre quotes $40-150 for a 30-90 minute remote interview; marketplace listings show focus groups and interviews from $133-350, and studies requiring professional credentials at $267-500+. Compare that with one hour on a mainstream panel at $1-4: two orders of magnitude apart.

The cost is hit rate. Screeners are unpaid and often longer than the session itself; a B2B screener may run fifteen questions covering company size, the budget you approve and which tools you use. Testers report qualification rates in the single digits to low teens on public projects. Paid interviews frequently require verification through a work email address, which rules out student accounts.

The realistic approach is to treat it like a low-frequency lottery: scan new projects for twenty minutes a week, apply where you genuinely fit, skip the rest, and do not reverse-engineer screeners. Gaming the profile may raise your hit rate briefly, but the first probing question in the live session exposes it, the incentive gets withheld and the account gets flagged.

Strengths

  • Session fees of $40-500, far above any survey panel
  • Incentive and format stated upfront
  • Paid via Tremendous within about 7-10 business days
  • Heavy B2B volume, which rewards genuine professional background

Weaknesses

  • Screeners are unpaid and often lengthy
  • Low qualification rate; most projects will not match you
  • Paid interviews often require work-email verification
  • Not stable income; weeks can pass with no match

Practical tips

  • Fill in job title, industry, company size and decision authority accurately; that is the B2B gate
  • Answer screeners honestly rather than inventing a role to get in
  • Show up on time once booked; no-shows get flagged and reduce future invitations
  • Watch time zones, since US studies often land in your late evening

Frequently asked

Is Respondent legit?

Yes. It is an established research marketplace with stated incentives and payouts through Tremendous. Note that screeners are unpaid and most applications are not selected, which is normal for this category.

Why do I keep failing screeners?

Usually a profile mismatch on company size, role or decision authority, then using a personal instead of work email. Guessing can occasionally work but falls apart under live questioning.

How long do payouts take?

Tremendous issues payment after attendance is confirmed, typically within 7-10 business days. If a researcher does not confirm, follow up through platform support.
