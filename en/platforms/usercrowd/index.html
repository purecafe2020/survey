UserCrowd

Micro usability tests: tiny fees, few geographic limits, useful for practice and spare change.

Deep research Overall score：3.2 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Key facts

Type
Micro tests: five-second, click and preference tests
Typical pay
About $0.20-1 per test
Session length
Ten seconds to two minutes
Payout
PayPal, from a $10 balance
Eligibility
124 countries
Payout timing
Processed within about 7 days

UserCrowd occupies the most granular layer: you see a screenshot and answer what you remember after five seconds, or which of two buttons you would click. A test takes seconds and pays $0.20-1.

It should not be treated as income, but it has two genuine uses. First, practice: building the habit of narrating a judgement while looking at a screen makes a twenty-minute UserTesting session much easier. Second, geography: coverage in 124 countries is the widest in this category, so regions that cannot register elsewhere can still do something here.

The ceiling is low. At roughly $0.20 a minute, a full hour yields a little over ten dollars and requires constant task switching. The lived experience is closer to turning spare minutes into pocket change.

If you sign up, frame it as a practice ground for learning how the industry works. Real money in paid research still sits in interviews and focus groups.

Strengths

  • 124-country coverage, the fewest geographic barriers
  • Tasks take seconds, do them anytime
  • Good practice for longer paid tests
  • Withdraw from $10

Weaknesses

  • Very low pay, roughly ten dollars an hour at best
  • Constant switching is mentally draining
  • Not an income source
  • Volume swings with client campaigns

Practical tips

  • Focus while doing them; quality scores matter
  • Use it to practise articulating a five-second judgement
  • Withdraw at $10 rather than accumulating
  • Treat it as a practice ground, not a time sink

Frequently asked

How much can you earn on UserCrowd?

About $0.20 a minute, so a little over ten dollars an hour at best. Practice and pocket change, not income.

Is it available in mainland China?

Coverage spans 124 countries and registration is open, but you need PayPal and volume depends on client campaigns.

How is it different from UserTesting?

UserCrowd is seconds-long micro tasks paying cents to a dollar; UserTesting is a full twenty-minute narrated test at $10-60. Different tiers entirely.
