Prime Opinion

A 2023-launched global survey app whose selling point is a very low cash-out floor and fast payouts.

International Overall score：4.0 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Prime Opinion only launched in 2023 but grew quickly on a simple proposition: an extremely low cash-out floor and near-instant payment. US users can withdraw from $1 to Venmo, Cash App or a bank account, or from $5 to PayPal, with withdrawals processed immediately. For people who quit when they cannot see money, that beats a better hourly rate on paper.

Its inventory is aggregated from several research companies, which means volume but also the usual side effects: plenty of screen-outs, sometimes after a long qualifying section. Reviewers have complained that the screener can run as long as the survey itself, and it is not an isolated complaint. A first withdrawal may also trigger an identity check that can involve biometric data, which privacy-sensitive users should weigh beforehand.

Do not expect much per hour. Third-party tests in 2026 put it at roughly $1.60 to $4.50 per hour with wide variance. The advertised "up to $5 a survey" is a ceiling, not an average, and the live counter on its homepage quoting an $11 daily average is a US figure that does not transfer to other markets.

Verdict: good for people who want immediate feedback and a light psychological load, poor as a primary income source, and not ideal for anyone uncomfortable with identity verification.

Strengths

  • $1 cash-out in the US with fast payouts and quick positive feedback
  • Available in about 71 countries
  • High survey volume, so there is usually something to do
  • Multiple rails: PayPal, Venmo, Cash App and bank

Weaknesses

  • Frequent screen-outs, sometimes after long qualifying sections
  • First payout may trigger an ID check involving biometrics
  • Low and highly variable effective hourly rate
  • Polarised Trustpilot reviews with a split between five stars and one star

Practical tips

  • Confirm which payout rails your country supports before investing time
  • Withdraw at the minimum rather than accumulating a balance
  • Abandon screeners that run unusually long; switching tasks beats grinding
  • Read the help-centre page on verification first if biometrics concern you

Frequently asked

Is the $1 cash-out real?

For US users, yes, via Venmo, Cash App or bank; the PayPal rail starts at $5. Other countries have different floors, visible on the redemption page after sign-up.

Can users in mainland China join?

It covers about 71 countries; mainland China is not among the standard supported markets and payout rails do not line up. Registering with a false location is not advisable.

Why am I asked for ID?

Identity checks are triggered on first withdrawals or by risk rules to prevent duplicate accounts and fraud. Some checks involve biometric data such as a face scan.
