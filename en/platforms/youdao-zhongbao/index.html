有道众包

NetEase Youdao's crowdsourcing arm, weighted toward language and text work.

China Overall score：3.4 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Key facts

Type
Language and text crowdsourcing
Operator
NetEase Youdao
Task types
Audio transcription, English dictation, proofreading, classical text entry
Payout methods
Alipay or WeChat Pay per platform notes
Cash-out minimum
From 10 yuan
Typical hourly rate
Roughly 10 to 20 CNY per hour, higher once skilled

Youdao Zhongbao's task structure resembles Souhuobang's, but the emphasis differs: it leans toward language processing. Transcription, English dictation, proofreading and classical text entry reward sensitivity to language rather than stamina.

That suits a specific crowd well. English majors, teaching students, writers and people already doing proofreading work ramp up far faster than general users. Rewards are labelled clearly by difficulty and length, so you know the payment before starting.

The hourly rate depends on fluency. A beginner may need 90 minutes to transcribe a ten-minute clip, landing under 10 CNY an hour; once the tools and conventions click, that can double. This is the key difference from surveys: there is a genuine learning curve, and time invested becomes skill.

Withdrawal starts at 10 yuan, a modest floor. Note that tasks are claimed competitively and release times are irregular, so turn on notifications. Quality standards are strict, and error rates above threshold get work sent back.

Strengths

  • Text and language work that suits people with relevant backgrounds
  • Rewards labelled clearly by difficulty
  • A real learning curve that raises pay with skill
  • Low 10-yuan withdrawal floor

Weaknesses

  • Beginners earn little until the learning curve flattens
  • Competitive claiming with irregular release windows
  • Strict quality checks that send work back
  • Limited total volume, so not a stable income

Practical tips

  • Practise on short clips to build transcription speed
  • Set up shortcuts and templates; the efficiency gap is large
  • Enable notifications to catch release windows
  • Self-check before submitting; rework costs more than slowing down

Frequently asked

Who is it for?

People with strong language instincts or proofreading background, such as English majors, teachers and editors. Complete beginners need to survive the learning phase first.

What is the withdrawal floor?

10 yuan, with channels and timing per the platform's own notes.

Why do tasks disappear so fast?

Batches are released in limited numbers. Enable notifications and be online during release windows.
