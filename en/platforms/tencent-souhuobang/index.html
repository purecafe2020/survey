腾讯搜活帮

Not surveys but data-labelling work: better rates, but it demands care and patience.

Souhuobang is not survey work. It is data-labelling crowdsourcing: annotating AI training data, transcribing audio, drawing bounding boxes on images and moderating content to rule. Tencent runs it for its own models and content governance, which is why volume is relatively steady and settlement is orderly.

The upside is that unit rates beat surveys clearly. Transcription is paid per clip, from a few mao to several yuan; image labelling is paid per image or per box, with complex work reaching ten or twenty yuan. With practice the effective rate reaches 15 to 20 CNY an hour, better than questionnaires, because it rewards skill and care rather than luck.

The cost is the entry barrier. You have to read the training docs and labelling specs, which is real upfront effort. Quality is enforced strictly, so bad submissions get rejected or penalised and directly reduce the work you can see. And tasks are competitive: popular batches are claimed within minutes, so timing matters.

Who it suits: people who type fast, can sit still, and are willing to learn the specs. Students, parents at home and freelancers wanting flexible hours do well. If you want money for random tapping, this will disappoint.

Also worth stating: real-name verification is required, and income counts as labour remuneration subject to individual income tax once above the threshold, usually withheld by the platform.

Strengths

  • Backed by Tencent with orderly, reliable settlement
  • Higher unit rates than surveys, reaching 15-20 CNY per hour with practice
  • Several task families to choose from
  • A low 10-yuan withdrawal floor

Weaknesses

  • You must learn the labelling specs first
  • Strict quality review; errors are rejected and penalised
  • Popular batches are claimed fast, so timing is not fully flexible
  • Counts as labour income with tax withheld

Practical tips

  • Spend an hour on the training docs first; it beats learning by rejection
  • Start with transcription, which has the simplest spec
  • Watch for batch release windows, often weekday mornings and evenings
  • Review rejection reasons after each batch; higher accuracy unlocks more work

Frequently asked

Is Souhuobang a survey site?

No. It is crowdsourced data labelling, transcription and moderation, priced per item rather than per questionnaire.

How fast are withdrawals?

Most tracks allow withdrawal from 10 yuan to WeChat Pay; timing follows the platform's stated rules.

Why do I see fewer tasks?

Usually reduced accuracy tightening your permissions, or missing batch releases. Fix your pass rate first.
