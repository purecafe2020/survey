百度众测

Test-flavoured tasks with an unusually low 0.1-yuan floor, though payouts settle weekly.

China Overall score：3.5 / 5 Verified：November 2026

From 0.1 yuan (5 vouchers), at 50 vouchers per yuan
Payout timing
Requested this week, paid the following Friday

Baidu Zhongce is positioned around testing: you help Baidu verify that product features behave correctly, with labelling and questionnaire work mixed in. Tasks therefore come structured as a scenario, a set of steps and a feedback requirement, which feels closer to a small job than to blind tagging.

Its payout mechanics are distinctive. Settlement is in vouchers at 50 vouchers to 1 yuan, with a minimum of 0.1 yuan (5 vouchers) to Alipay balance. The Alipay account must match the real-name details on your Baidu account. Requests made this week are paid the following Friday, holidays permitting, and tax rules apply.

Earnings sit at the lower-middle end: voucher counts per task are modest and income comes from volume. Its real value is variety. If product testing and bug hunting interest you, the work is considerably less dull than questionnaires.

One caveat: voucher withdrawal is open to mainland China residents only, and the function is occasionally paused for system changes, which the site announces. Do not leave a large voucher balance sitting in the account.

Strengths

  • An extremely low 0.1-yuan withdrawal floor
  • Varied tasks, with product testing that is less tedious than surveys
  • Operated by Baidu with published rules
  • Clear voucher maths at 50 vouchers to 1 yuan

Weaknesses

  • Weekly settlement rather than instant payout
  • Modest voucher counts, so the hourly rate is low
  • Withdrawal limited to mainland China residents
  • The withdrawal function is occasionally paused

Practical tips

  • Start with easy tasks to learn how vouchers are priced
  • Watch official announcements and avoid paused withdrawal windows
  • Match your Alipay real-name details to your Baidu account
  • Withdraw at regular intervals rather than hoarding vouchers

Frequently asked

How do vouchers convert?

50 vouchers equal 1 yuan; the minimum withdrawal is 5 vouchers (0.1 yuan), with the after-tax amount being what actually arrives.

When does the money arrive?

Requests made in a given week are paid the following Friday, allowing for holidays.

Can overseas users withdraw?

No. Voucher withdrawal is limited to mainland China residents and requires matching Alipay verification.
