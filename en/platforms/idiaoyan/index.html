爱调研

Web, app and WeChat entry points, with points converting straight into WeChat Pay balance.

Among Chinese paid-research platforms, idiaoyan has the lightest entry point: it works inside WeChat, with no app install and no desktop required. For people who only have fragments of time, the lower the friction, the less likely they are to give up halfway.

The mechanism is points. You complete surveys, points become cash, cash lands in WeChat Pay. The site advertises a 1-yuan first withdrawal, which is the lowest floor in this category: two or three surveys on day one can already produce a payout, unlike panels that require accumulating 50 yuan first.

Inventory is consumer research: FMCG, personal care, baby products, food and drink, cars and financial products, asking about usage habits and brand awareness. Surveys run 5 to 20 minutes with rewards from a few mao to a dozen yuan, and the higher-value ones tend to be long questionnaires or projects requiring detailed background information.

Realistic income needs saying plainly: ignore the "thousands a month" posts. At a sustainable pace of 30 to 60 minutes a day, expect roughly 100 to 400 CNY a month, with occasional spikes during large studies. It covers a phone bill or a few coffees, not a salary.

Two caveats. First, quotas are finite, and popular segments such as urban women aged 25-35 burn through theirs quickly, so earlier is better. Second, withdrawals require real-name verification, and multiple accounts under one ID are treated as a violation.

Strengths

  • Runs inside WeChat, the lowest-friction entry available
  • 1-yuan cash-out gives fast positive feedback
  • Steady new inventory every day
  • Cash lands in WeChat Pay, no extra account needed

Weaknesses

  • Low unit price, roughly 5 to 15 CNY per hour
  • Popular demographic quotas fill fast, causing screen-outs
  • Real-name verification required, one account per person
  • Survey quality varies, with some tedious long forms

Practical tips

  • Complete your profile, especially city, household and spending habits
  • Mornings match better than evenings
  • Redeem at 1 yuan instead of holding a balance
  • Refuse any "internal task" that asks you to add a personal WeChat or pay a deposit

Frequently asked

Is the 1-yuan withdrawal real?

The site advertises a 1-yuan floor for new users; the actual tiers are shown in your account. First withdrawals may require real-name verification.

How much per month?

At 30 to 60 minutes a day, most people land between 100 and 400 CNY, with spikes during large studies. It is not a stable income source.

Why no points after finishing?

Either the quota filled and you were screened out, or the completion time was too short to count. Such cases are usually uncompensated.
