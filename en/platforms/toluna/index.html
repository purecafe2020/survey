Toluna Influencers

A long-running global community with wide coverage and low barriers, but rewards skew toward vouchers.

International Overall score：3.5 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Key facts

Type
Survey and poll community (points-based)
Coverage
60+ countries
Payout methods
Mainly vouchers and gift cards; cash in some markets
Cash-out minimum
Varies by market, usually several thousand points
Payout speed
Several days to two weeks after redemption
Typical hourly rate
About $1 to $3

Toluna is one of the oldest names in the category, present in more than 60 countries, and it mixes surveys with community polls, topic discussions and prize draws. It reads more like a participation-driven research community than a pure earning tool.

It has historically been more accessible in Asia than some US-only panels, with localised redemption options in some markets. Note, though, that redemption rules differ sharply between markets: several regions pay mainly in vouchers or coupons rather than cash, and point thresholds are set locally, so guides written for one country do not transfer.

Expectations should stay modest. Third-party reviews consistently place it at $1 to $3 per hour, and the points-to-value conversion is opaque because different task types award points on different scales, so the realised rate often undershoots the impression.

If you want cash and efficiency, Toluna is not the first pick. If you want a wide-coverage backup to slot in third or fourth, it works.

Strengths

  • Available in more than 60 countries
  • Polls and community features break up the survey grind
  • Long operating history
  • Localised redemption in some markets

Weaknesses

  • Rewards skew to vouchers, with limited cash options
  • Opaque points conversion often yields a lower real rate
  • Rules vary widely by market, so shared guides mislead
  • Prize draws are easily mistaken for earnings

Practical tips

  • Check your market's redemption page to see whether cash is even available
  • Keep prize draws out of your earnings log
  • Only take surveys with a stated reward; skip ambiguous point tasks
  • Use it as a backup rather than a primary platform

Frequently asked

Can I withdraw cash from Toluna?

It depends on your market. Some countries offer vouchers and coupons only. Confirm on the redemption page after sign-up.

How do points convert to money?

There is no published universal rate; different tasks award different point values, so the real value is whatever the redemption tiers show.

Can users in mainland China register?

Toluna covers 60+ countries but runs them independently, so availability and redemption for mainland China depend on what the official site actually offers.
