Userlytics

Mostly usability tests with some interviews, wider country coverage, good as a backup.

Deep research Overall score：3.6 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Key facts

Type
Usability tests, short interviews, surveys

About $5-90 per task depending on complexity
Session length
Mostly 10-30 minutes
Payout
PayPal
Eligibility
Broader country coverage, including non-US markets
Screener
Per-project qualification survey, unpaid

Userlytics occupies the backup slot: less usability volume than UserTesting, lower interview fees than Respondent, but broader country coverage, so non-US participants occasionally get projects. That wider reach is not common in this category.

Rewards are tiered by complexity, from around five dollars for a click test up to 30-50 for a full narrated test, with interviews occasionally reaching 90. Do not expect frequency; volume depends on your market and client campaigns.

The product is rougher than the leaders: invitations arrive late, briefs are occasionally vague and support is slow. If your time is expensive, treat it as third in line; if you are starting out and want practice, the barrier really is lower.

One practical habit: keep it open alongside UserTesting and compare rates before committing. The same client-side test can pay double on one platform versus the other.

Strengths

  • Wider country coverage than US-only platforms
  • Low barrier, useful for practice
  • Mixed formats: tests and interviews
  • PayPal settlement

Weaknesses

  • Unstable volume; still limited outside the US
  • Weaker product experience and slow support
  • Lower ceiling on per-task pay
  • Occasionally vague briefs that lead you off-target

Practical tips

  • Enable email alerts so time-limited invitations are not missed
  • Read ambiguous briefs twice before recording
  • Compare against UserTesting before deciding it is worth it
  • Complete your profile, especially role and device details

Frequently asked

Is Userlytics worth joining?

As a backup, yes. Do not make it your primary; it works best to fill gaps once UserTesting is running.

How does it pay?

Through PayPal, with timing depending on the project's review cycle.

Can users in China participate?

You can register, but volume depends on clients targeting your market, so opportunities are modest.
