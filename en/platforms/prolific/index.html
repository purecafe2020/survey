Prolific

An academic and commercial research marketplace that publicly enforces a minimum hourly rate.

Prolific is not really a survey site in the usual sense. It is a research marketplace where university labs, AI teams and market research firms recruit participants and pay by the hour. What makes it different is that the pay standard is written into platform rules: researchers must pay at least £6 (about $8) per hour, and £9 to £12 is recommended. It is the only mainstream platform that publishes and actually enforces a floor.

For participants that means two things. The reward and the estimated time are shown before you start, so you can calculate the effective hourly rate yourself. And because researchers are recruiting to a sample rather than fishing, you rarely hit the classic half-hour screen-out that wastes your time; Prolific has the lowest disqualification rate in this category. The trade-off is supply: some weeks bring a dozen studies, others bring none that match your profile.

The real bottleneck is access. Prolific uses a waitlist rather than first-come-first-served, and says invitations follow whatever demographic mix researchers currently need, so some people are invited within hours and others wait months. After an invitation you still have to pass identity verification, and the company has disclosed that roughly 55% of invited people complete onboarding. It is also open only to most OECD countries, excluding Turkey, Lithuania, Colombia and Costa Rica.

If you are in a supported country and verified, Prolific is one of the few realistic options with a transparent hourly rate. Cash out to PayPal from £6/$6, usually within 24 hours, though your first four withdrawals must be 72 hours apart (the clock resets if you change your PayPal address), after which it is once a day. If a researcher has not reviewed your submission within 21 days, it is auto-approved and paid. Use a PayPal account that matches your registered country, otherwise you can trip a security hold.

Strengths

  • The only mainstream platform with a published, enforced hourly floor
  • Very low screen-out rate compared with consumer panels
  • Reward and expected duration are shown before you start
  • Low cash-out threshold (about £6/$6) with fast PayPal payouts

Weaknesses

  • Waitlist is invitation-only and can take months
  • Limited to most OECD countries, so many regions cannot join compliantly
  • Study supply is uneven week to week
  • ID verification puts some privacy-conscious users off

Practical tips

  • Fill in your demographic profile completely and consistently to get matched more often
  • Never create a second account; duplicates are permanently banned
  • Check once a day at a fixed time and rely on notifications rather than refreshing
  • Cash out only to a PayPal account matching your registered country

Frequently asked

Can I use Prolific from mainland China?

No, not compliantly. Prolific is limited to most OECD countries and requires verification that matches your location. Using a fake identity or proxy to appear local breaches its terms; the usual outcome is a ban with the balance forfeited.

How much can you earn on Prolific in a month?

It depends on how many studies you are matched to. The hourly floor is guaranteed by platform rules, but volume is not in your control. Most active participants land somewhere between tens and a couple of hundred pounds a month; treat it as supplemental income.

Why is my submission still pending?

Researchers review submissions. If a researcher has not responded within 21 days the submission is auto-approved into your balance, and you need at least £6/$6 to withdraw.
