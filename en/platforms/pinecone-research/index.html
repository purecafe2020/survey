Pinecone Research

An invitation-only panel with a flat per-survey fee and very low disqualification; the hard part is getting in.

International Overall score：3.9 / 5 Verified：November 2026

Pinecone Research is refreshingly blunt: a flat $3 per survey, minimal disqualification. No points conversion, no tiers, no draws. You finish the questionnaire and you get paid, which is rare enough in this industry to explain its reputation.

The obstacle is entry. It is invitation-only and the public sign-up is usually closed. Invitations typically come from three places: the site's own waitlist, referrals from other research programmes, and occasional ad placements. Wait times range from weeks to more than a year with no predictable pattern.

Once you are in, the experience is smooth. Survey volume is moderate but steady, each taking 10 to 20 minutes, and the screen-out rate is officially under 5%, so you rarely waste a session. At $3 a survey the effective rate lands around $9 to $15 per hour, which is upper-middle for this category.

One warning: there is a thriving market of paid "Pinecone invitation codes" and "registration services". They are all scams. Pinecone does not sell invitations and does not release access through paid third parties.

Strengths

  • Flat fee and fully transparent rules
  • Under 5% screen-out rate, so time is rarely wasted
  • Effective rate of roughly $9 to $15 per hour
  • Predictable 10-20 minute surveys

Weaknesses

  • Invitation-only with an often-closed public entry and unpredictable wait
  • Limited total volume, so not a primary income
  • Only a handful of countries
  • Many fake "invitation code" scams in circulation

Practical tips

  • Use the official waitlist page and ignore everything else
  • Watch for referral emails from legitimate research programmes
  • Any paid "invitation code" is a scam
  • Keep your profile current after joining to stabilise volume

Frequently asked

How do I get an invitation?

Through the official waitlist, referrals from legitimate research programmes, or official ad placements. There is no reliable trick and no purchasable code.

Is it really a flat $3?

In most markets yes, roughly $3 per standard survey, though longer studies may carry different terms stated in the invitation.

Is it available in mainland China?

Its markets are mainly the US, UK and Canada; mainland China is not a standard market.
