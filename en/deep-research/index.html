How to use this table

If you are new, start with usability testing: lowest barrier, no résumé-style verification, twenty minutes a session. Use it to rehearse the two skills that matter, narrating while using and delivering on time. Once that feels natural, move to interviews and focus groups with a profile that now looks credible, and your hit rate changes.

If you already have a professional background in procurement, IT, healthcare, law or finance, go straight to credentialed studies. At $267-500+ a session it beats a hundred usability tests.
