China focus groups: where the work is

This is not a nationwide market; the city decides the opportunity. The same ninety-minute session may pay 300 CNY in Shanghai, while a tier-three city offers 150 with only one eligible person around, which means you get picked.

Verified：November 2026

One counter-intuitive fact: lower-tier studies pay respectably but are hard to staff. If you genuinely live in a tier-three or four city, you are a scarce sample in many projects, so your odds beat competing with thousands of people in Shanghai for one seat.

What distinguishes the four tier-one cities

Beijing: policy, finance, technology and automotive headquarters. B2B and specialist studies dominate, covering public affairs, state-owned procurement and healthcare policy, and depth interviews often pay more than focus groups.

Shanghai: the bridgehead for foreign FMCG, personal care, automotive and finance. Consumer focus groups are densest here, especially baby, beauty and food and drink.

Guangzhou: strong in FMCG and personal care, with automotive and appliances as well. Cantonese speakers form a separate quota in some studies.

Shenzhen: technology, consumer electronics, EV owners and cross-border buyers. Digital product and hardware experience studies are easiest to fill here.

Why the newer hubs are worth targeting

Six cities have grown noticeably over the past two years: Chengdu, Wuhan, Hangzhou, Xi'an, Nanjing and Chongqing. Three forces explain it: brands expanding regionally need samples there, local field houses cost less and will take smaller projects, and tier-one samples are saturated so clients push outward.

Specific strengths: Chengdu (southwest consumer hub, food and leisure), Wuhan (dense universities, plenty of students and young professionals), Hangzhou (e-commerce and digital economy, heavy online shoppers), Xi'an (northwest regional centre, tourism and automotive), Nanjing (strong education and healthcare, specialist audiences), Chongqing (large population, pronounced lower-tier characteristics).

Getting into local recruiter databases

There is no public listing like Respondent. Opportunity lives inside respondent databases. Four routes, in order of effectiveness:

  1. Register with a local field house: search for the city name plus focus group company, market research or 焦点小组, find the fieldwork firm's site or phone number and complete their long intake form. This is the most reliable route.
  2. Recruiter WeChat contacts: once in a database, supervisors message you directly, which is far faster than public posts and they remember people who turn up.
  3. WeChat official accounts: search for focus group recruitment plus a city name; many field houses post continuously.
  4. Street intercepts: malls, communities and baby stores, common for FMCG and infant studies.

Whichever route you take, legitimate recruitment never charges a fee and never asks you to recruit others for payment.

Settlement is usually cash or WeChat transfer on the day. A minority pay through a corporate account requiring ID and bank details, which happens only with established fieldwork firms and comes with a confidentiality agreement. Anyone asking you to pay first is running a scam.

Online groups: the way around geography

Since 2023, online groups have become a mainstream format: sixty to ninety minutes on Tencent Meeting or Zoom, open nationwide, paying 150-400 CNY. Their value is greatest outside the big cities, since you no longer travel three hours for a 200-CNY session.

Four things decide whether you survive the opening: join ten minutes early, turn the camera on (required by most studies), have a quiet room and stable connection, and prepare a one-line introduction, since moderators usually open by asking your occupation and household. Fail any of these and you can be removed before the discussion starts.

A practical checklist for the day

  • The day before: confirm the address and travel, reply to the confirmation message and put your ID in your bag.
  • On the day: arrive fifteen minutes early; arriving more than ten minutes late usually means cancellation and a no-show record.
  • Bring: ID for verification and a phone for payment. Do not bring a companion.
  • During: answer honestly, do not talk over people and do not go silent, because moderators record participation.
  • After: confirm the fee arrived before leaving, and ask for a receipt on the spot if you need one.

No-show records are shared among Chinese field houses, and one missed session can follow you for six months.
