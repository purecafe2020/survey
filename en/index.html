The top two sell judgement; the bottom two sell time. The gap is two orders of magnitude, and it is not effort, it is scarcity. So the first question is never "which pays most" but "what do I have that most people do not".

Five gates

All four routes pass the same five gates

GateWhat it isWhere most people fail
1. RegionWhether the platform serves your countrySignup works, work never arrives
2. IdentityID, selfie, face verificationA VPN, or a name that does not match the ID
3. AssessmentA one-shot test, sample or screening callNot knowing it can only be taken once
4. DeliveryQuality score, deadlines, instructionsThe score slips and work vanishes silently
5. PayoutName match, tax form, FX costThe payout account does not match the registration

Only gate three is about ability. The other four are about not sabotaging yourself, which is why half of this site is about those and not about earning more.

Our position

Only methods that pay out and stay compliant

A lot of Chinese-language content in this space teaches proxy switching, multiple accounts, filling in for others and buying leaked links. We publish none of it, not from moralising but from three practical outcomes:

  • You will not get paid. Risk systems cross-check IP, device, payout account and profile consistency; mismatches mean withheld incentives and forfeited balances.
  • The account dies. One flag ends future invitations, which burns the long-term ticket.
  • It is legally exposed. Misrepresenting identity to obtain payment is fraud in several jurisdictions, not grey-area side work.

We do cover the red-line category, but only as "why not to": paid-to-click, watch-ads-for-money, fake orders and paid reviews, and anyone selling a guaranteed pass. They share one property: under a dollar an hour, in exchange for your account, a deposit, or legal exposure.
