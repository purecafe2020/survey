Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it does not, and how third-party advertising uses cookies. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

1. Information we collect

This is a static site with no registration, no login and no membership system, so we do not actively collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or identity documents.

Server and CDN logs, recorded automatically by the hosting provider, may contain IP addresses, browser type, requested paths and timestamps for security and traffic measurement. Those logs are not linked to any personal identity.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set our own tracking cookies. Pages may include third-party services, such as analytics or advertising, that set cookies or similar identifiers for measuring traffic and serving ads.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. The pages remain readable; only aggregate statistics become less accurate.

3. Third-party advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display advertising through Google AdSense. Please note:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Further detail is available in Google's advertising technologies documentation.
  • Where required by law, Google applies restricted data processing (RDP) for visitors in certain US states and honours Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. No extra configuration is needed here, and you can enable GPC in your browser at any time to limit personalised advertising.

4. External links

We link to platform websites and regulator pages. Following a link takes you off this site, and the destination's privacy policy applies. We do not use hidden tracking parameters in outbound links.

5. Children's privacy

This site is aimed at adults, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their information. Most survey platforms require participants to be 18 or older.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no account data, access, correction or deletion requests usually do not apply. If you believe content references your personal information, for example a quoted public comment you want removed, contact us via the Contact page and we will review it. Rights of EU and UK users under GDPR and of California residents under CCPA are respected where applicable.

7. Changes

The date at the top of this page is updated whenever the policy changes. Material changes will be flagged on the home page.
