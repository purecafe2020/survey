First, the nature of it: this is not a survey and not an interview. Your output is a judgement. You rank two model answers, score a piece of Chinese, rewrite what fails so it reads like it began in Chinese, and explain why you decided that. The platform turns those judgements into training signal, which means your written explanation is the deliverable.

The Chinese track has three shapes: reviewing EN-ZH translations for meaning, flow and register; rating and ranking Chinese output while flagging clumsy measure words, forced chengyu and manual-like phrasing; and rewriting what falls short. The role page specifically calls out mixed Simplified and Traditional conventions and a register that quietly turns into a manual, which is exactly the gap a native reader closes and a model does not.

Read the pay bands carefully. The FAQ lists language work from $25-40 an hour, general $25-50, coding $40-150+, and STEM or licensed professional work $40-125+. The claim that many contributors make $100k-200k a year is real, but it describes coders and STEM specialists working near full time for years. On the Chinese language track, at a $40 ceiling and a few hours a week, tens to hundreds of dollars a month is the realistic range.

There is one real gate: the Starter Assessment. About an hour, one to two for specialist tracks, and the official FAQ states plainly that it can be taken once with no retakes. It tests the three things above: can you see the problem, can you name the cause, can you fix it. So preparation is not memorising platform rules; it is practising a hundred words of English that state what is wrong, why, and how to fix it.

On region, be honest with yourself. As of November 2026 the site no longer publishes a country list and the FAQ does not either. But earlier versions of the homepage stated that it accepted contributors from the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, New Zealand and Australia only, and third-party tracking points the same way. The practical read: you can register and sit the assessment, but whether projects keep coming afterwards is not decided by how well you answer. Which is why this should not be your only platform.

One line never to cross: no VPN region switching, no fake addresses, no bought accounts. Identity verification compares documents, selfie and network environment; a mismatch means a ban and a frozen balance, and everything before it is void. The compliance cost here is the same as on research platforms: region and identity must be genuine.

Frequently asked

Can I register from outside the six countries?

The site publishes no country list today and the signup flow does not block you, so technically you can register and sit the assessment. But earlier homepage versions named the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, New Zealand and Australia only, and third-party tracking points the same way, so steady work after approval is a separate question. What you must not do is spoof it with a VPN or a fake address: verification compares documents against network data, and the result is a ban and a frozen balance.

Does the Chinese track require a degree?

The role page states no degree is required; it asks for native-level Chinese, comfort across Simplified and Traditional, and clear written English. General work is listed as bachelor's or equivalent experience, which is a different bar. No degree does not mean no filter: the assessment is the filter.

Can the Starter Assessment be retaken?

No. The official FAQ states it can be taken once, with no retakes or second chances, and that submissions are reviewed for thoroughness and accuracy rather than speed. That is why you practise first instead of treating the first attempt as a trial run.

How long does approval take?

The FAQ says you will be emailed within a few days, and that approval grants immediate access to projects. It also says the application volume makes individual responses impossible, so prolonged silence is a realistic outcome rather than a bug.

How do I get paid?

PayPal only, in USD, arriving within a few days of your request in the dashboard. The PayPal email must match your registration email. Non-US contributors also file a W-8BEN. Withdraw at the threshold rather than leaving a balance on the platform.

My queue is empty after approval. Is my account dead?

Not necessarily. Work is project-scheduled and fallow periods are normal. But when quality scores slip, work disappears quietly and is rarely explained. The way to tell them apart is whether new projects open to you and whether you can still take higher-tier assessments. If nothing appears for a long stretch, move your hours elsewhere instead of waiting.
