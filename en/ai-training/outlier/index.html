Outlier · Most projects

An hourly AI-training platform with broad project coverage and supply that follows training cycles.

Mainly English markets, some projects wider

Per project cycle, often weekly or fortnightly
Access
Application, skills test, ID verification

Outlier is Scale AI's contributor-facing platform, doing the same job as the rest of this category: humans judge model output and rewrite the weak parts. Projects range from a two-minute preference judgement to long coding tasks that require you to explain a solution.

Its character is volume and speed. A dozen projects may be open at once, and each lives for a short window before the training run finishes and the queue closes. So you will see five projects available one week and none the next; that is the platform's rhythm, not your account.

Rates are tiered by difficulty, from around $15 to well over $50 an hour on the same platform. The variable is not typing speed but whether you can do what most applicants cannot, which is where a professional background multiplies the rate.

Whether it is worth it depends on tolerance for that rhythm. It is a poor primary income and a decent burst-work supplement, provided you do not plan your bills around it.

Why it works

  • The largest project volume in this category, so a first task is easy to find
  • Hourly pay makes the economics more transparent than per-task pricing
  • Specialist tracks in coding, healthcare and law pay materially more
  • Backed by Scale AI, with a traceable payment record

What you accept

  • Short project lifespans with no warning when they end
  • Skills tests and ID checks gate entry
  • Strict quality review; rejections reduce future allocation
  • Unsupported countries cannot participate

How to prepare

  • Treat the skills test seriously; it decides your allocation
  • Track two or three projects at once instead of waiting for one to end
  • Read the rubric twice; it matters more than speed
  • Log weekly hours and payments, since this is contractor income

Frequently asked

How is Outlier different from DataAnnotation?

Same category, different shape: Outlier has more, shorter projects billed hourly; DataAnnotation has longer tasks with a higher ceiling. Most people register for both.

Why did the projects disappear?

A training run finished or the project closed. It is normal and not an account restriction; wait for the next batch or switch platform.

Can users in mainland China participate?

Not in the standard supported markets, and it requires ID verification plus a local payment method. Registering with a false region is not advisable.
