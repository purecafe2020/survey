Mindrift · Widest coverage

Project-based AI training with wide domain and country coverage, best for people with a specialist background.

Mindrift does the same work as Outlier and DataAnnotation but organises it differently: it recruits per project, with a stated domain, duration and acceptance criteria, and pays once the work is accepted. For people who prefer project work to task grinding, that is more controllable.

Coverage is the advantage. Ninety-plus domains mean a nurse, a tax accountant or a structural engineer can be matched to something relevant rather than dropped into a generic pool. Non-English markets are included, which is unusual in this category.

The cost is pace: a project can take weeks from application to payout, with tests and onboarding in between. That makes it a poor fit for anyone who needs money this week, and a good fit for someone with a real specialism willing to spend an afternoon on a test.

Two things decide whether a project is worth starting: a written acceptance standard, and a stated payout timeline. If both are vague, expect friction at the end.

Why it works

  • 90+ domains, so a specialist background actually matches
  • Project-based payout usually comes with clearer acceptance criteria
  • Broader country coverage, including non-English markets
  • Operated by Toloka, with a more formal process than small crowdsourcing shops

What you accept

  • Long cycles: weeks from application to payout
  • Fewer concurrent projects than hourly task platforms
  • Tests and onboarding take unpaid time
  • Scope sometimes shifts mid-project

How to prepare

  • Read the acceptance criteria and payout terms before applying
  • Take the domain test seriously; it decides access to the higher-paying projects
  • Keep the project brief and acceptance notes, which are your only evidence in a dispute
  • Apply to two or three projects to smooth out the cycles

Frequently asked

How do Mindrift and Toloka relate?

Mindrift is Toloka's brand front-end for AI-training projects, running on the same underlying crowdsourcing network.

What background do I need?

Nothing for general tasks, but higher-paying projects require verifiable professional background such as a licence, industry experience or a degree.

How long until payment?

After acceptance, per the project terms, commonly a few weeks. Confirm the terms before starting.
