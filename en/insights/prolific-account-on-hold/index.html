Prolific Account on Hold: Causes and How to Resolve It

A hold looks alarming and usually is not fatal. It means the platform's fraud checks flagged something, and it will not clear until a human reviews the specific mismatch.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

What a hold actually is

A hold freezes study access and withdrawals while the platform verifies something. It is a review state, distinct from a permanent ban, though a review can end in one if the mismatch is deliberate.

Your balance is not deleted on a hold. It stays attached to the account and moves once the review clears.

The five triggers

TriggerWhat the check sees
ID does not match the profileName, date of birth or country differs from the verification document
PayPal name mismatchThe payout account belongs to a different legal name
Country inconsistencyIP history, device locale and registered country disagree
Duplicate account signalsSame device, payout method or address linked to another account
Unusual activityA burst of studies at implausible speed, or shared-device usage

The first three account for most holds. All three are avoidable with boring consistency: one legal name, one country, one payment account, everywhere.

The only route that works

Contact participant support through the platform and answer exactly what they ask. Typically that means a clear photo of an identity document, confirmation of your registered address, and confirmation of the PayPal account name.

What does not work: opening a second account to start fresh (that converts a hold into a ban), contacting researchers directly, or sending documents that contradict your registered details.

How long it takes

Usually days, occasionally a couple of weeks if documents need re-verification. Support cannot promise a date, and chasing daily does not accelerate it.

If you believe the hold is mistaken, say so once with specifics, such as a travel period that explains an IP change. Specific explanations are reviewed; generic frustration is not.

Preventing the next one

  • Keep the name on your ID, your Prolific profile and your PayPal account identical.
  • Cash out to a PayPal account registered in the same country as your profile.
  • Use one device on your normal home connection; avoid VPNs during sessions.
  • Never create a second account, even after a rejection or a long waitlist.
  • If you move country, update the profile before taking more studies.

Frequently asked

Do I lose my earnings during a hold?

No. The balance stays on the account and becomes withdrawable once the review clears, provided the account is not banned.

Can I open a new account to get around it?

No. Duplicate accounts are the fastest route from a hold to a permanent ban, and balances are usually forfeited.

How long do holds last?

Typically days, sometimes up to a couple of weeks if documents need to be re-verified. There is no guaranteed timeline.
