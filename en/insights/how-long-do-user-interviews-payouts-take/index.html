How Long Do User Interviews Payouts Take?

The money is agreed before you join the session, but it is released after the researcher confirms you attended. That single step explains nearly every payout delay.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

The payout flow

  1. You attend the session.
  2. The researcher marks attendance as confirmed.
  3. The incentive is released to your account.
  4. You choose PayPal or a gift card and withdraw.

Steps one to three are where time is spent. Everything after is fast.

Typical timelines

StageTypical duration
Researcher confirms attendanceSame day to a week
Incentive releasedImmediately after confirmation
PayPal settlementA few business days
Gift card deliveryUsually faster than PayPal

If two weeks pass with no confirmation, that is the point to contact support rather than waiting longer.

Tax forms before the first payout

Depending on where you live and how much you have earned, the platform may require tax information before releasing funds, typically a W-9 for US participants or a W-8BEN for everyone else.

This is routine rather than suspicious, and it applies to most research platforms once earnings pass a threshold. Filling it in early avoids discovering it at the moment you want to withdraw.

The most common blocker: name mismatch

Payment is wired to the name on your account. If your PayPal account is registered to a spouse, a business name or an old legal name, the transfer fails or gets held.

Fix it before your first session: the name, address and country on the research profile and the payment account must agree.

If the payment is late

Contact support with the study name, session date and the incentive amount. Ask whether attendance has been confirmed; that is usually the missing step, not the payment itself.

Do not message the researcher repeatedly. One calm follow-up through support is the norm, and it resolves the overwhelming majority of cases.

Frequently asked

How long do payouts take on User Interviews?

Typically a few days to two weeks after the session, once the researcher confirms attendance. The confirmation step is the usual delay.

Do I need to submit a tax form?

Often yes, once earnings reach a threshold. A W-9 or W-8BEN is standard and does not affect whether you get paid, only when.

What if my PayPal email is different from my profile email?

Use a payment account registered to the same legal name and country as your profile. A mismatch is the most common reason transfers stall.
