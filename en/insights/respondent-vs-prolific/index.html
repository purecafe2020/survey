Respondent vs Prolific: Fees, Access and Who Should Join

People compare these two because both pay better than panels, but they are not substitutes. One is a per-session marketplace, the other is an hourly research panel with a published floor.

Access: the real differentiator

Prolific gates on country. It serves most OECD countries plus a handful of others, and uses a waitlist that invites roughly 13% of applicants based on the demographic mix researchers need.

Respondent gates on profile and email. It accepts registrations widely, but the well-paid B2B work requires a work-domain address and a role that matches. Consumer studies are open to anyone in a supported market.

Which pays more per hour

Respondent, on a per-session basis, and by a lot: $40-150 for an hour against roughly $8-12 on Prolific. But Prolific's advantage is predictability, because the floor is enforced and calculated from actual median completion times rather than the researcher's estimate.

So Respondent has the higher ceiling and Prolific has the higher floor. For most people the sensible order depends on whether they can pass a work-email check.

Time to first payment

Prolific is faster. Cash out from £6/$6 to PayPal, usually within 24 hours, and unreviewed submissions auto-approve after 21 days.

Respondent settles through Tremendous about seven to ten business days after the researcher confirms attendance. Larger amounts, longer wait.

Who should join which

  • Employed in a recognisable function: Respondent first, because that is where the $200 sessions are.
  • In a supported country but outside B2B: Prolific first for steady hourly income.
  • Neither fits (unsupported country, no work email): consumer interviews and usability tests, which gate on neither.

Running both

They are separate companies, so there is no conflict, and most serious participants use both. The only rule is one account per platform: duplicates are the leading cause of bans in this industry.

Keep the same facts on both. Cross-platform consistency is what makes a profile credible when a reviewer looks.

Frequently asked

Which pays more, Respondent or Prolific?

Respondent per session, often by a factor of ten. Prolific has a guaranteed hourly floor, which Respondent does not.

Can I use both from the same country?

Yes, provided your country is supported. Prolific requires an eligible country; Respondent is more open but withholds B2B work without a work email.

Which is easier to get into?

Respondent registration is immediate, though qualifying for paid sessions is not. Prolific requires surviving a waitlist that invites a minority of applicants.
