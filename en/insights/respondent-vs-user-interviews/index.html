Respondent vs User Interviews: Which to Join First

These are the two platforms most people compare, and the honest answer is that they solve different problems: one maximises fee per session, the other maximises sessions per month.

Which pays more

Respondent, on average, and by a clear margin on B2B and specialist work. Its marketplace ranges reach $350 for focus groups and $500+ where credentials are required.

But a higher ceiling is not the same as a higher month. If Respondent has two studies matching your profile and User Interviews has fifteen, the second platform may still pay you more in aggregate.

Which is easier to get into

User Interviews, for most people. It onboards immediately with no waitlist, and its mix leans toward consumer and light SaaS topics where a normal profile qualifies.

Respondent's better-paid work often needs a work email and a role, so it rewards people already employed in a relevant function.

Run both, in this order

The practical sequence: register for User Interviews first, use it to learn how screeners behave and how sessions are run, then add Respondent once you can describe yourself clearly in a profile.

Both allow the same person. What neither allows is a second account on the same platform, which is the fastest route to a permanent ban.

How payouts differ in practice

User Interviews pays by PayPal or virtual gift card shortly after attendance is confirmed, and lets you set a minimum fee so low-value invitations do not crowd your inbox.

Respondent settles through Tremendous, typically seven to ten business days after the researcher confirms. The delay is longer, the amounts are larger, and the per-project visibility is better.

Who each suits

  • Respondent: employed professionals, specialists with credentials, anyone who can pass a work-email check.
  • User Interviews: everyone else first, including freelancers, students and consumer profiles.

If you only have appetite for one, start with volume and graduate to fees.

Frequently asked

Is Respondent or User Interviews better paid?

Respondent pays more per session, especially in B2B. User Interviews may pay more per month because it offers more studies.

Can I use both platforms?

Yes. They are separate companies. Using multiple accounts on the same platform is what gets people banned.

Are both legitimate?

Yes. Both have published incentive terms and verifiable payout records, and both leave screeners unpaid.
