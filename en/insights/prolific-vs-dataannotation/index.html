Prolific vs DataAnnotation: Two Very Different Jobs

People compare these because both are often described as "the good ones". They are not the same kind of work, and choosing between them is really a question about how you handle uncertainty.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

Side by side

AttributeProlificDataAnnotation
Work typeResearch studies, surveys, AI evaluationWriting, grading and correcting model output
Pay£6-12/hr equivalent, enforced floor$40-150/hr for coding, $15-30 general

The difference that matters

Prolific's floor means a bad week still pays something. DataAnnotation's ceiling means a good week pays more than a month of Prolific, and an empty week pays nothing at all.

So the choice is not about rate. It is about whether you can absorb a week with zero income in exchange for a rate that is five to ten times higher when work exists.

Entry cost is asymmetric

DataAnnotation asks for an unpaid test, commonly an hour, and rejects a large share of applicants. Prolific asks for a waitlist that may never move, plus ID verification if it does.

Both are cheap in money and expensive in patience. Do not take the DataAnnotation test on a rushed evening; it is the single highest-leverage hour in this whole category.

Why running both is sensible

The two smooth each other out. Prolific provides the floor and the habit of checking daily; DataAnnotation provides the upside when a project is running. Neither takes the other's work.

The failure mode is treating DataAnnotation like a job. It is a contract queue. When the queue empties, that is not a signal to panic or to email support repeatedly.

Which to start with

If you need reliable small income, start with Prolific and add DataAnnotation when you have an hour to invest in the test. If you have a technical background and can tolerate gaps, take the DataAnnotation test first, because the ceiling is far higher.

Frequently asked

Which pays more, Prolific or DataAnnotation?

DataAnnotation per hour, often by a factor of five. Prolific pays more per month for most people because the supply is steadier.

Do I need coding skills for DataAnnotation?

Not for the entry tier, but the headline $40-150 an hour requires a verifiable technical or professional background.

Can I do both?

Yes, they are unrelated companies and different work. Many people use Prolific as the floor and DataAnnotation as the upside.
