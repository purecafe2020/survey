Paid Research in Brazil: Platforms, Language and Payouts

Brazil is the standout in Latin America: a large consumer market, an active fintech sector, and one of the few non-OECD countries the big research platforms explicitly include.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

What is open

Prolific includes Brazil in its supported countries, which is unusual and useful. Toluna covers Brazil, UserCrowd reaches it through its 124-country coverage, and local panels exist for Portuguese-language consumer research.

US-centric panels such as Survey Junkie and Branded do not operate in Brazil, so ignore any guide that lists them for Brazilian residents.

The Portuguese advantage

Brazilian consumer research is conducted in Portuguese, and clients need Brazilian respondents specifically. If you are a native speaker, that combination — large market, hard-to-fill Portuguese quotas, foreign clients paying in dollars — is the best position in the region.

Brazilians who also speak English can pick up international B2B and technology studies, which pay considerably more.

What it pays

CategoryTypical pay
Local consumer panelsModest, in BRL or vouchers
Prolific studiesFloored hourly rate in USD/GBP
Usability tests$10-60 per test
International interviews$40-350 per session

As in India, nominal USD amounts go a long way locally, which makes the upper tiers of this market genuinely worthwhile and the bottom tier not worth the time.

Payout and tax

PayPal is the common route, with the usual 3-4% conversion drag into BRL. Gift cards often exist but are less useful outside the country, so cash conversion is usually the right choice.

Brazilian tax rules apply to the income; platforms do not withhold. Keep records in BRL at the exchange rate on the day you receive, which is what the tax authority expects.

What to avoid

Two traps specific to this market. First, sites advertising high payouts in Portuguese are frequently lead-generation pages, not research panels; check for a named company. Second, some "Brazilian panel" sites are aggregators of foreign surveys that will screen you out instantly on country, wasting weeks.

Frequently asked

Does Prolific work in Brazil?

Yes, Brazil is on its supported list, which is one of the few non-OECD inclusions. Confirm on the help page before registering.

Do US panels like Survey Junkie work in Brazil?

No. They operate in the US, Canada and Australia. Guides that list them for Brazil are wrong.

Is Portuguese required?

For Brazilian consumer research, yes. English opens international B2B and technology studies, which pay more.
