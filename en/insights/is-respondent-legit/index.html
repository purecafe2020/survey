Is Respondent Legit? Fees, Payouts and the Catch

Respondent is legitimate, pays well, and rejects most people who apply. All three things are true at once, and understanding the third is what stops you wasting evenings on it.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

What it is

A research recruitment marketplace operated by a named US company. Researchers post studies with the format, length and fee stated; you apply, complete a screener, and if selected you are booked into the session. Payment runs through Tremendous, typically seven to ten business days after attendance is confirmed.

These are the highest fees in the category, and they are stated before you apply rather than revealed afterwards.

The catch, stated plainly

Screeners are unpaid and often long. A B2B screener can run fifteen questions about company size, budget authority and tools used. Selection rates are low, typically single digits to low teens on public studies. Better-paid work requires a work email, verified against LinkedIn or a corporate team page.

So the realistic expectation is: apply to roughly twenty studies to attend one. At $150 a session, that is still an excellent hourly rate on the hours you actually work.

How to tell it apart from a scam

Three checks, all quick. It never charges you to join. The operating company is named and US-registered. And the payment flow is one-directional: money arrives, you are never asked to send any.

Anything claiming to offer "Respondent access" or "guaranteed selection" for a fee is a third party selling something that does not exist.

Getting more out of it

  • Fill in job title, industry, company size and authority precisely. That is what the matching runs on.
  • Answer screeners honestly, including the unflattering options; consistent refusals are how real profiles look.
  • Apply within 24 hours of a study appearing, before quotas close.
  • Show up on time. A no-show is remembered and reduces future invitations.

Frequently asked

Does Respondent actually pay?

Yes, through Tremendous, typically 7-10 business days after the researcher confirms attendance. Payout records are widely reported.

Why do I keep getting rejected?

Almost always a profile mismatch or quota closure, then the work-email gate for B2B studies. It is not a personal judgement.

Are there any fees?

None. Registration is free and the money always flows toward you. Any charge indicates a third party, not Respondent.
