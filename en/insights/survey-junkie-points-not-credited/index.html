Survey Junkie Points Not Credited: What to Do

Two very different things look identical from the outside: a survey that screened you out, and a survey you completed but did not get paid for. Knowing which one happened decides whether there is anything to dispute.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

Screen-out versus completion

A screen-out happens early, when your profile does not match the quota. No points, and nothing to appeal: that is the design.

A completion is when you reach the final page and see a thank-you screen. If points do not appear after that, you have a legitimate dispute.

Why credit can be delayed

  • Sync lag of a few minutes during peak hours.
  • The client's own system has not confirmed the completion yet.
  • Your session lost connection at the last step.
  • The survey flagged a quality issue, such as inconsistent answers or implausible speed.

Give it an hour before assuming anything. Most delays resolve without action.

What evidence to keep

EvidenceWhy it matters
Screenshot of the completion screenProves you finished rather than dropped
Survey ID or nameLets support locate the record
Date and timeNarrows the log search
Points value shownEstablishes what you are owed

Without a completion screenshot, disputes become your word against a log. Take the screenshot every time; it costs two seconds.

How to dispute properly

Use the platform's own support channel, include the evidence above, and state the facts in three lines: what you completed, when, and what is missing. Ask for the points to be credited, not for an explanation.

What reduces success: emotional framing, claiming every survey shorted you, or opening several tickets for the same survey.

What usually gets resolved

Genuine sync failures and client-side confirmation gaps are usually credited once support looks. Quality flags are usually upheld, because the client's data is the record of truth.

If a single site repeatedly fails to credit legitimate completions, that is a pattern worth acting on: stop using it rather than spending hours disputing.

Frequently asked

How long should points take to appear?

Usually within minutes. Give it an hour before treating it as missing.

Do I get points for being screened out?

No. Screen-outs are unpaid on Survey Junkie and most panels; a few sites pay a small consolation, but this one generally does not.

Can I dispute a rejected completion?

Yes, with a completion screenshot and the survey ID. Quality-based rejections are usually upheld, but genuine sync failures are often credited.
