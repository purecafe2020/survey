Paid Research in Canada: Platforms, Pay and Payouts

Canada is close to the US in treatment and occasionally better, because Canadian clients need Canadian samples and there are far fewer people competing for them.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

US-only panels are the ones to watch: several accept sign-ups from anywhere but then show no inventory, which looks like a shadow ban and is not.

What it pays

Consumer panels: roughly $2-5 an hour, similar to the US but with a smaller study pool. Research platforms: Prolific at its enforced floor, UserTesting at $10-60 a test, Respondent and User Interviews at $40-350 per session for interviews.

French-language studies are a real advantage. Canadian clients often need Quebec samples specifically, and bilingual participants face much less competition.

Currency and payout

Most platforms pay in USD, so Canadian participants absorb conversion either into CAD or through PayPal's spread. Where a platform offers CAD gift cards, the effective value is usually better than a converted PayPal transfer.

Tax treatment follows Canadian rules rather than US ones: the income is taxable, and no platform will withhold on your behalf.

The French-language edge

If you speak French, say so in every profile field, and mark it as a working language rather than a school subject. Bilingual studies pay the same as English ones but are dramatically easier to qualify for because the pool is small.

This is the single highest-return change a Canadian participant can make.

Frequently asked

Do US survey sites work in Canada?

Some do, some do not. Survey Junkie and Branded both include Canada; others accept registration but show no studies.

Are French studies worth targeting?

Yes. Quebec samples are hard to fill, so bilingual participants qualify far more easily for the same fees.

Is the pay in USD or CAD?

Mostly USD, converted at payout. A CAD gift card is often worth more than a converted PayPal transfer.
