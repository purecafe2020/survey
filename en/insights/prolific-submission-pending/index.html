Prolific Submission Pending: When You Actually Get Paid

You finished the study, the timer stopped, and the money did not appear. That is normal. Prolific pays on approval, not on completion, and there is a rule that protects you if the researcher goes quiet.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

Pending is not a problem, it is a stage

After you submit, the study sits in "awaiting review" until the researcher approves it. Only then does the reward move into your balance, and only a balance can be withdrawn to PayPal from £6 or $6.

Most researchers approve within a few days. Some batch-approve weekly. A few go quiet, which is why the platform has a fallback.

The 21-day rule

If a researcher has not reviewed your submission within 21 days, it is automatically approved and paid. This is the single most useful thing to know on this platform: a silent researcher cannot hold your money indefinitely.

Practical consequence: if a submission is pending past three weeks, check whether the reward has appeared rather than assuming you need to chase it.

Rejections are appealable through support, and they do affect your standing, so a wrong rejection is worth contesting with specifics.

Return versus rejection

If you realise mid-study that you do not fit, return it. Returning is the polite exit: the researcher gets the slot back, you get nothing, and your record stays clean.

Abandoning without returning leaves the submission open, and it will eventually be rejected. The difference matters because returns are not held against you, while a pattern of rejections is.

Chasing a payment without damaging the account

Message the researcher once, politely, through the platform, after about a week. State the study name and submission date, and ask whether anything else is needed.

Do not send repeated messages, do not email outside the platform, and do not threaten. Researchers are usually individuals with day jobs; a single calm nudge gets a response far more often than three impatient ones.

What to do while waiting

Nothing. That is the point of the 21-day rule. Take more studies if they appear, keep your balance records for tax, and withdraw whenever you cross the threshold rather than accumulating.

Frequently asked

How long does a Prolific submission stay pending?

Until the researcher approves it, or 21 days, after which it is auto-approved and paid.

Can I speed up approval?

Only by messaging the researcher once, politely. There is no platform mechanism to escalate before the 21-day mark.

Does returning a study hurt my score?

No. Returning is the correct exit when you do not fit, and it is not penalised the way a rejection pattern is.
