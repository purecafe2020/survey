Paid Research in India: What Pays and What Doesn't

India is one of the few non-Western markets with real coverage on international platforms, and the reason is straightforward: a very large English-speaking population and a huge technology sector.

Verified：November 2026 About 2 min read

What is open

Prolific includes India in its supported countries, which makes it one of the few markets outside the OECD with access to an enforced hourly floor. UserCrowd's 124-country coverage includes it, Toluna covers India, and the domestic panel and crowdsourcing industry is active.

Usability testing platforms also recruit Indian participants, though volume is lower than in the US or UK.

Where the volume actually is

Technology is the standout. Software companies globally need developers, product managers and IT decision-makers in India, and those interviews pay properly.

The honest arithmetic

A $3 survey is worth very little in a US context and considerably more in India, so purchasing-power comparisons matter more than nominal rates. A $60 usability test is a good afternoon; a 40-cent micro test is not.

So the correct strategy in India is to skip the bottom of the market entirely and concentrate on Prolific studies, usability tests and B2B interviews.

The payout step that trips people up

Most platforms pay via PayPal, and receiving international payments into an Indian PayPal account has rules that change more often than participants expect, including restrictions on holding balances.

Before investing weeks of time, confirm: (1) that you can receive the payment method the platform uses, (2) the route from that wallet to your bank, and (3) any fees. Test with the smallest possible withdrawal first.

Language and profile

Strong English is an asset here and should be stated explicitly: English fluency widens eligibility to international B2B studies, which pay several times what consumer panels do. Regional language ability is occasionally relevant for consumer research and worth listing.

Frequently asked

Can users in India join international platforms?

Some, yes. Prolific explicitly supports India, UserCrowd covers 124 countries, and Toluna operates there. Verify per platform before investing time.

What pays best in India?

B2B and technology interviews, then usability tests, then Prolific studies. Consumer panels are the weakest tier.

Any payout issues?

Possibly. PayPal's rules for receiving international payments into Indian accounts change, so confirm the route and test with a small withdrawal first.
