Which platforms work in which countries

The same platform can be open in the US and closed in Japan. This page covers the variable people ignore most, geography, which determines whether you have any work at all.

Verified：November 2026
Three hard rules: your registered region must match where you actually live; your payout account name and region must match your registration; never spoof a region with a proxy. Violating any of them means withheld payment plus a ban, with balances forfeited. Supported-country lists change without notice, so check each platform's help page before registering.

By region

North America (US, Canada)

The richest supply: consumer surveys, B2B interviews, usability tests and diaries all work here, at the highest rates. US-only panels such as Survey Junkie, Branded Surveys and InboxDollars exist for this market alone.

The right structure in North America is deep research as the main line with panels filling gaps: Respondent and User Interviews for the big fees, panels for idle minutes. Not the reverse.

United Kingdom

The UK is underrated: Prolific pays in GBP, Branded Surveys and Swagbucks both operate, and YouGov is a domestic company. UK respondents are often grouped separately in European studies, so competition is lighter than in the US.

Watch the payout: GBP to local currency through PayPal costs 3-4% in conversion, which stings on small amounts, so accumulate before withdrawing.

Continental Europe (DE, FR, NL, ES, IT, Nordics)

Prolific, Toluna, YouGov and Prime Opinion operate across most of Western Europe; Swagbucks has a German site. Nordic markets have fewer projects but higher fees because samples are scarce.

Language is the gate: local studies require native-level interviews, and English-language studies favour UK and North American participants. Speaking the local language is the real advantage here.

Australia and New Zealand

Survey Junkie supports Australia, and Prolific, Swagbucks, Toluna and UserCrowd all work. ANZ respondents are folded into "English market" studies, but quotas are smaller than in the US and UK, so first-come matters more.

The upside is time zone: you can catch Asian-hours studies and often see fresh US quotas in the morning.

Asia-Pacific (JP, KR, SEA, South Asia)

Prolific supports Japan, South Korea, India and Indonesia; UserCrowd covers 124 countries; Toluna and Prime Opinion have partial coverage. India, with its large English-speaking population, sees real B2B and technology volume.

The barrier is language and hours rather than eligibility: English interviews demand fluency and often land in the North American night.

Mainland China

International platforms are effectively unavailable compliantly: Prolific is limited to its listed countries, Survey Junkie and Branded concentrate on North America, and Prime Opinion's 71 markets exclude the mainland. Proxy spoofing breaches terms, forfeits balances and gets accounts banned.

Domestic options are survey panels (idiaoyan, yidiaowang, shoujiang) and data crowdsourcing (Tencent, Alibaba, Baidu, Youdao), converting to roughly 5-20 CNY an hour. Offline focus groups are a separate track, covered in the city list.

Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan

Hong Kong is on Prolific's supported list and can access GBP and USD studies. For Taiwan and Macau, check each platform's actual registration page rather than trusting guides.

The real value here is time zone plus language: Asia-hours projects are reachable, and some Chinese-language market research can be done in Mandarin.

Latin America, Middle East, Africa

Coverage is patchy: Prolific lists Brazil, Chile, Mexico, Egypt and Qatar, while UserCrowd's 124-country reach is the most permissive. Most other platforms are closed.

Projects here skew to local consumer goods and fintech; fees are mid-range but competition is light, so take what appears rather than waiting on it.
