Expert Call Rates: Why One Hour Pays $100 and Another $1,000

In the same industry and the same hour, a newly promoted director may earn $200 while a purchasing lead at a regional distributor earns $500. Seniority is not the only variable.

Verified：November 2026

Four typical bands

Rare specialistThe only person who knows a specific approval process or legacy system$800-1,000+

Bands shift by industry: healthcare, energy and semiconductors sit above consumer goods because the decisions and the compliance are heavier.

Why senior executives often cannot take the call

Compliance teams at listed companies routinely restrict employees from taking paid external consultations, particularly where unpublished operating data is involved. The result is an inversion: the bigger the company and the more senior the role, the less likely participation is allowed.

That leaves room for middle managers and regional roles, who know the operational detail without the same compliance exposure. It is why a purchasing lead at a second-tier firm is often the most in-demand profile.

The buyer sets the ceiling

Investment firms pay most, because one conversation can inform a nine-figure decision. Consultancies come next, usually bundled into a client engagement. Corporate strategy teams pay least but may book more often.

The same CV is therefore worth very different amounts across networks. Before accepting a first invitation, ask who the end client is.

How to describe yourself into a higher band

Buyers pay for answers others cannot give. Your description should state three things: the specific function (not "supply chain" but "ran a 12-person logistics team covering southern China"), decision position (do you sign, and up to what value), and the unique vantage point you have seen and others have not.

Frequently asked

Can I take expert calls without an executive title?

Yes, and middle managers and regional roles are core recruitment targets. Buyers want real experience at a specific vantage point.

Why do identical roles get different fees?

Different buyers: investment firms pay most, corporate strategy least. Industry matters too, with healthcare and energy above consumer.

Is $1,000 an hour real?

Sometimes, when you are the only source on a specific process and the buyer is making a large decision. Most calls land at $200-400.
