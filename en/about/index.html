About

A reference site on overseas remote income that sells no courses, recruits no referrals and promises nothing.

What we do

This site documents publicly available information on overseas remote income, split by what you sell: judgement (AI training, expert calls), skill (transcription, subtitles, voice recording), data (interviews, focus groups, surveys), and the category we tell you to avoid. Every track lands on the same gates: how much it pays, how the money arrives, and when it does not.

What we do not do: register accounts for you, sell invitations, offer "inside channels", or encourage proxy spoofing.

Where the content comes from

Each entry cross-checks three source types: official help pages and terms for thresholds, settlement cycles and eligible regions; repeated third-party test reports for effective hourly rates and screen-out ranges; and public user reports on payout delays and appeal experiences. Where a figure cannot be verified, it is marked "check the official page" rather than invented.

Platform rules change quickly, so each page carries the month it was verified. If a number has gone stale, tell us.

Our position

There are no affiliate links here and ranking does not follow commissions, which also means we have no incentive to promote whoever pays most. We make no income promises. Survey earnings depend heavily on region, profile and platform supply, and claims of stable four-figure monthly income do not hold up.

Disclaimer

This is information and experience, not legal, tax or investment advice. Platform rules, rates and availability change without notice and the official pages prevail. Your relationship with any platform is governed by its terms; this site does not participate, guarantee or pursue claims on your behalf.
